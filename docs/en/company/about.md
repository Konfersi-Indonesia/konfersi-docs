Konfersi delivers metocean, climate and environmental intelligence for offshore, coastal, energy and infrastructure projects, through digital platforms and expert consulting.

## Who we are

PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant (Konfersi Indonesia) is an oceanography, meteorology and climatology consulting company. We specialise in metocean data analysis, climate analysis, ocean modelling, high-resolution wind data, and research and innovation.

The industries we support include environmental assessment, oil and gas, coastal development and renewable energy. Our analyses are calibrated and validated against established global datasets such as ERA5, CMEMS, FES2022 and CMIP6.

## What we do

Our work is built on three pillars.

### 1. Consulting & data

Project-based studies carried out by our team:

- metocean studies and design criteria
- climate analysis
- ocean data analysis and time-series analysis
- ocean modelling
- high-resolution wind modelling (WRF)
- underwater noise modelling for environmental impact assessment (EIA / AMDAL)

### 2. Courses

Practical training in ocean and climate data:

- **Konfersi Course Series**: structured video courses organised in modules, with a certificate
- **Web Series**: self-paced learning with quizzes
- **Private Course**: training tailored to your team

Announced topics include ocean physics reanalysis data processing, CMIP6 climate data analysis, Argo float data processing, and tide analysis and prediction, all in Python.

## Our story

Konfersi has operated as a brand since around 2021. In 2024 it was incorporated as a limited liability company (Perseroan Terbatas). Our team works across Indonesia and Japan, with our headquarters in Jakarta Pusat.
