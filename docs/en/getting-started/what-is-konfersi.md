Konfersi delivers metocean, climate and environmental intelligence for offshore, coastal, energy and infrastructure projects. You can work with it through our digital platform, through expert consulting, or by learning the methods yourself in our courses.

Konfersi is the brand of **PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant** (Konfersi Indonesia), an oceanography, meteorology and climatology consulting company based in Jakarta. We specialise in metocean data analysis, climate analysis, ocean modelling, high-resolution wind data, and research and innovation. Read more in [About Konfersi](../company/about.md).

## Who Konfersi is for

Teams in these industries use our work:

- Environmental assessments, including baseline studies for EIA/AMDAL
- Oil and gas
- Coastal development
- Renewable energy

## Three ways to work with Konfersi

### 1. The metocean digital platform

This is one account that opens several apps:

- **Console** is where you manage projects, plans and your profile.
- **Lab** is a browser-based workbench for the project you have open.
- **Accounts** handles sign-up and sign-in for every Konfersi app.
- **Mission Planning** is the first analysis module to be released publicly. It is rolling out from the end of October 2026. See [Mission Planning](../using-konfersi/mission-planning.md).

[Products & surfaces](products-and-surfaces.md) explains what each app does and where to find it.

### 2. Consulting and data

We also run project-based studies, including metocean studies and design criteria, climate analysis, ocean data analysis, ocean modelling, high-resolution wind modelling (WRF), underwater noise modelling for EIA/AMDAL, and time-series analysis. To discuss a study, [contact us](../support/contact-support.md).

### 3. Courses

The Konfersi Course Series, Web Series and Private Courses teach practical metocean and climate data work in Python. Topics include reanalysis data processing, CMIP6 climate data, Argo float data, and tide analysis.

## Where the data comes from

Our models and analyses are calibrated and validated against widely used global datasets, including ERA5, CMEMS, FES2022 and CMIP6. [Metocean data sources](../using-konfersi/metocean-data-sources.md) describes each one.
