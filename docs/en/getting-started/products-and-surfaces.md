Konfersi is a set of apps that share one account. You sign in once, and the same session works in the Console and on the other Konfersi sites. This page explains which app does what.

## At a glance

| Surface | Address | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Website & docs | [konfersi.com](https://konfersi.com) | Learn about services and courses, and read these docs |
| Accounts | [accounts.konfersi.com](https://accounts.konfersi.com) | Create an account, sign in, reset your password, complete your profile |
| Console | [app.konfersi.com](https://app.konfersi.com) | Enrol in courses, pay for orders, and manage your profile and organization |

## Accounts

Accounts is the single sign-in service for Konfersi. When you open the Console without an active session, you are sent to Accounts to sign in and then returned to where you were.

- [Create an account](create-account.md)
- [Sign in](sign-in.md)

## Console

The Console is your home base. From the side menu you can open:

- **Dashboard**: an overview of your account and recent activity.
- **Courses**: the course **Marketplace**, **My Courses** and your **Certificates**. See [Courses](../using-konfersi/courses.md).
- **Community**: article discussions and a members-only Q&A. See [Community](../using-konfersi/community.md).
- **Organization**: your team's members and settings, if you belong to an organization. See [Organizations](../using-konfersi/organizations/index.md).
- **Payment & Billing**: your order cart and transactions. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md).
- **Profile & Settings** and **Support**.

More Konfersi products will be added to the Console over time.

## Consulting and courses

Not everything happens in the apps. Konfersi also runs consulting studies and offers courses. Both are described on [konfersi.com](https://konfersi.com) and in [What is Konfersi?](what-is-konfersi.md).
