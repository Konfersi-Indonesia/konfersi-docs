Konfersi is a set of apps that share one account. You sign in once, and the same session works in the Console, in Lab and on the other Konfersi sites. This page explains which app does what.

## At a glance

| Surface | Address | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| Website & docs | [konfersi.com](https://konfersi.com) | Learn about services and courses, and read these docs |
| Accounts | [accounts.konfersi.com](https://accounts.konfersi.com) | Create an account, sign in, reset your password, complete your profile |
| Console | [app.konfersi.com](https://app.konfersi.com) | Create and manage projects, plans, add-ons, payments and your profile |
| Lab | [lab.konfersi.com](https://lab.konfersi.com) | Work inside a project in a browser-based workbench |
| MCP | mcp.konfersi.com | Coming soon: connect your own AI agent to Konfersi |

## Accounts

Accounts is the single sign-in service for Konfersi. When you open the Console or Lab without an active session, you are sent to Accounts to sign in and then returned to where you were.

- [Create an account](create-account.md)
- [Sign in](sign-in.md)

## Console

The Console is your home base. From the side menu you can open:

- **Dashboard**: an overview of your work, recent projects and recent activity.
- **Lab Project**: create, open, edit and delete projects, and choose plans and add-ons.
- **Courses** and **Community**: live and self-paced courses, article discussions and a members-only Q&A.
- **Organization**: your team's shared projects and members, if you belong to an organization.
- **Payment & Billing**: your order cart and transactions.
- **Profile & Settings**, **Console Guides** and **Support**.

Every piece of work in Konfersi belongs to a **project**, and each project has its own plan and quotas. See [Console & projects](../using-konfersi/console-projects.md).

## Lab

Lab is the metocean workbench for the project you have open. It runs in your browser with an editor-style interface, and it shows your project's plan and quota usage. You open Lab from a project in the Console. See [Lab workspace](../using-konfersi/lab-workspace.md).

## Mission Planning

Mission Planning is the first analysis module Konfersi is releasing publicly. It runs inside a Console project and estimates operational downtime for a location and period. It is rolling out from the end of October 2026. See [Mission Planning](../using-konfersi/mission-planning.md).

## MCP (coming soon)

The Konfersi MCP server will let AI agents on your own machine work with Konfersi's agentic AI, curated datasets, external sources and your Lab projects. It is not available yet.

## Consulting and courses

Not everything happens in the apps. Konfersi also runs consulting studies and offers courses. Both are described on [konfersi.com](https://konfersi.com) and in [What is Konfersi?](what-is-konfersi.md).
