**Console Guides** is the Console's welcome page. It helps you organise and monitor your Lab work from one dashboard. It opens by itself the first time you sign in to the Console, as long as you don't have a project yet. You can open it again at any time from **Console Guides** at the bottom of the side menu.

## Explore the Console

The guide greets you by name and offers three starting points. Each card has a main action and a skip option.

| Card | What it's for | Main action | Skip option |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explore Our Dashboard** | Get familiar with the Console layout and find everything you need to manage your workspace. | **Take a Tour** starts the dashboard tour. | **Skip Tour** |
| **Set up your Profile** | Change your profile picture and personalise your personal and organisation information. | **Settings** opens your profile. | **Skip** |
| **Create your first project** | Set up your first project, explore the Console's features and start working with Lab. | **Create** opens the new project form. | **Skip** |

Each skip option takes you to the **Dashboard**.

<scalar-callout type="info">Choosing any skip option, **Settings** or **Create** marks the welcome guide as done for your account, so it won't open by itself again on this browser. You can still reopen it from **Console Guides** in the side menu.</scalar-callout>

To learn how to create a project step by step, see [Console & projects](console-projects.md#create-a-project).

## Take the dashboard tour

**Take a Tour** opens the **Dashboard** and walks you through it in five steps. Each step highlights one part of the screen.

1. **Welcome to Konfersi Console**: the side menu is the Console's main menu. From here you reach the **Dashboard**, **Projects Management** and your plan settings in **Manage Plan**.
2. **Here's Your Workspace**: a quick overview of your workspace in one place, including your active projects, the points you've used and the artefacts you've generated.
3. **Stay in the Loop**: your latest activity, so you can keep track of project updates and important actions.
4. **Keep Learning**: our latest articles, tutorials and product updates, to help you get the most out of the platform.
5. **Recent Projects**: the project you opened most recently. If you don't have one yet, select **Create New Project** to get started.

### Moving through the tour

- Select **Next** to go to the next step, or **Back** to return to the previous one. On the last step, select **Finish**.
- Select **Skip** to leave the tour at any point.
- A bar along the bottom of each step shows how far through the tour you are.

While the tour is running, the rest of the page doesn't respond to clicks. Use **Next**, **Back**, **Skip** or **Finish** to move on. Finishing or skipping the tour marks the welcome guide as done for your account.
