**Community** in the Console side menu has two parts:

| Part | Who can see it | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Article Discussions** | Public, on Konfersi articles | Comment on and vote for articles from the Konfersi blog |
| **Member Q&A** | Signed-in members only | Ask other members and the Konfersi team, and answer their questions |

## Article Discussions

Each article from the Konfersi blog has a discussion thread. **All discussions** lists them; **Joined** shows the ones you commented on or voted for.

- Search, filter by topic, and sort by **Latest activity**, **Most discussed**, **Top voted** or **Newest articles**.
- Open a discussion to vote on the article, read the comments, and add your own with **Post comment**. Select **Read full article** to open it on the blog.
- You can vote on comments and delete your own.

Comments here are public, so leave out anything confidential.

## Member Q&A

Member Q&A is only visible to signed-in Konfersi members and isn't shown on the public website.

### Ask a question

1. Select **Ask a question**.
2. Write a **Title** with one specific question. As you type, **Similar questions** may already answer yours.
3. Add **Details**: what you're working on, what you tried, and what happened. Put code or logs in a code block (a line of three backticks before and after) to keep them readable. You can add photos.
4. Add up to five **Tags**, such as `extreme-value` or `python`.
5. Select **Post question**.

Name the dataset, location and period you're using, and leave out client names and anything confidential.

### Find and answer questions

Filter with **All**, **Unanswered**, **Open**, **Solved** or **My threads**, search, or filter by tag. Answers and comments from staff carry a **Konfersi team** badge.

- Vote on questions and answers.
- Write an answer under **Your answer** and select **Post answer**. Reply to answers with comments.
- If you asked the question, select **Mark as accepted** on the answer that solved it. The question is then marked **Solved**.

The Konfersi team can lock a thread. A **Locked** thread can still be read, but not answered.

### Need a private answer?

For billing, account or project-specific problems, use a [support ticket](../support/support-tickets.md) instead. On your own question you can also select **Ask support**: Konfersi copies the question into a private ticket, links it to the thread, and replies in **Support** and by email.
