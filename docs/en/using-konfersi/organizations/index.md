An organization brings a team together in the Console. Its members can work on every project shared with it, owners manage who belongs, and the organization's details can appear on each member's profile.

## Getting an organization

Organizations are set up by the Konfersi team. To start one, open **Support → New ticket**, choose **Sales Support**, and use the **Organization inquiry** template. Tell us your organization name and website, who should be the first owner, roughly how many people will join, and whether you need single sign-on. See [Support tickets](../../support/support-tickets.md).

If you don't belong to an organization yet, the **Organization** page in the Console explains this and offers **Contact sales**.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything a contributor can do, plus invite and remove members, change roles, edit the organization's details, and manage email domains, join requests, single sign-on and the activity log |
| **Contributor** | Work on every project shared with the organization, see members and shared projects, and leave at any time |

An organization always keeps at least one owner. The last owner can't become a contributor or leave until someone else is made an owner.

## Ways to join

- **Invitation**: an owner invites your email address. You accept from the email link or from the **Organization** page. See [Members & invitations](members-and-invitations.md).
- **Verified email domain**: if the organization has verified your work email's domain, you may join automatically or by asking an owner, depending on the rule the owners chose. See [Organization settings](settings.md#email-domains-and-joining-rules).
- **Single sign-on**: if your organization has turned on single sign-on, you can sign in with your company account. See [Organization settings](settings.md#single-sign-on).

Pending invitations and your join requests are listed under **Your invitations and requests** on the **Organization** page.

## Find your way around

Open **Organization** in the side menu. If you belong to more than one organization, use **Switch organization** to change between them. Each organization has these sections:

| Section | Who sees it | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | Everyone | About text, recent shared projects, and details such as your role, member count, type, sector, size and location |
| **Members** | Everyone | Members and their roles; owners also invite people and manage pending invitations |
| **Shared Projects** | Everyone | Every Lab project shared with the organization |
| **Shared Courses** | Everyone | Courses you are enrolled in |
| **Settings** | Owners | Organization profile, email domains, join requests and single sign-on |
| **Activity** | Owners | A permanent record of changes to the organization |

## Your profile and the organization

When you join an organization, your profile's organization details (name, type, sector, size, website and location) come from the organization and stay up to date when an owner changes them. Your role, such as your job title, stays your own. You can switch back to typing these details yourself at any time. See [Your profile](../../account/profile.md#organization-information).

## Leaving an organization

On the **Overview** section, select **Leave organization**. You lose access to the projects shared with it, and an owner can invite you again later. Your profile keeps the organization details it had, which you can then edit yourself.
