## Shared projects

When a Lab project is shared with an [organization](index.md), every member of that organization can open and work on it, with the same access as a collaborator you invite one by one. Members who join later get access too, and members who leave lose it.

The **Shared Projects** section lists every project shared with the organization, with its owner. The **Overview** section shows the most recent ones.

### Share a project

Only the project's owner can share it, and only with an organization they belong to.

1. Open the project from **Lab Project → My Projects** and select **Manage Project Access**.
2. Under **Organizations**, choose the organization and select **Share**.

You can also share a project from the start: when you [create a project](../console-projects.md#create-a-project), choose the organization under **Create as**. You stay the owner, and every member of the organization can open it.

### Stop sharing

In **Manage Project Access**, select **Stop sharing** next to the organization. Its members then lose access, unless they were also invited to the project themselves.

## Shared courses

The **Shared Courses** section lists the courses you are enrolled in, with their status (**Awaiting payment** or **Enrolled**). Sharing course seats across an organization isn't available yet, so each person enrols on their own. See [Courses](../courses.md).
