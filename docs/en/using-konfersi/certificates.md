When you complete a course, Konfersi issues a certificate in your name. You find all of them under **Courses → Certificates** in the Console.

## How you get a certificate

Konfersi issues a certificate automatically for courses that award one:

- **Self-paced courses**: when you mark the last lesson as done in the lesson player. See [Follow the lessons](courses.md#follow-the-lessons).
- **Live courses**: after the course team records your attendance.

When it's issued, you get a **Certificate issued** notification in the Console and a **Your certificate is ready** email. Your enrolment in **My Courses** also shows **Your certificate is ready** with **View certificate**.

## Your certificates

**Certificates** lists every certificate issued to you with its course, status, issuer, date and certificate ID. If you don't have one yet, it shows **No certificates yet.** with **Browse courses**. Open one to see the certificate and its **Details**:

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Recipient** | Your name as it appears on the certificate |
| **Certificate ID** | A unique ID in the form `KNF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` |
| **Issued** | The date it was issued |
| **Valid until** | The expiry date, or **Does not expire** |

Each certificate has a status: **Valid**, **Expired** (past its **Valid until** date) or **Revoked**. A revoked certificate can no longer be shared or downloaded.

## Share a certificate

Every certificate has a public verification page. From a certificate in the Console:

- **Copy link** copies the page address.
- **Share…** opens your device's share options.
- **Add to LinkedIn profile**, when shown, opens LinkedIn to add the certificate to your profile.
- **Open public page** shows the page exactly as others see it.

## Download a certificate

Under **Download**, choose **Download PDF** for printing or **Download image (PNG)** for posting. If the file is not ready, wait a moment and try again.

## Verify a certificate

Anyone can check a certificate without signing in. Open its link (it looks like `https://io.konfersi.com/c/KNF-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`). The page shows one of:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified certificate** | Konfersi issued it and it is valid. The page shows the recipient, course, issuer, certificate ID, issue date and expiry |
| **Expired** | It was genuine but is past its **Valid until** date |
| **Revoked** | Konfersi withdrew it. Only the ID, the issuer and the revocation date are shown |
| **Not found** | No certificate matches the link. Check that the ID is complete |

If the page says **Temporarily unavailable**, try again in a moment.

## Privacy

The verification page shows only what verification needs: your name, the course, the issuer and the dates. It never shows your email or account details. Certificate IDs are random and cannot be guessed, and the pages are hidden from search engines, so a certificate is only seen by people you share its link with.

If a detail on your certificate is wrong, contact [support](../support/contact-support.md).
