Konfersi runs live and self-paced courses. You find them under **Courses** in the Console side menu:

- **Marketplace**: every course on offer.
- **My Courses**: the courses you have enrolled in, with their join links.

## Browse courses

**Marketplace** lists each course with its format, schedule and starting price. Use the filter to show all courses or only live ones.

| Format | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Self-paced** | Start any time once your enrolment is confirmed |
| **Live online** | Scheduled sessions held online |
| **In person** | Scheduled sessions at a venue |
| **Hybrid** | Online and in person |

Open a course to see **About this course**, the **Schedule** (times are shown in the course's time zone), the **Instructor**, **Frequently asked questions** and the **Tickets** on sale. A ticket may show how many seats are left, or be marked **Sold out**, **Sales closed** or **Opens soon**.

## Enrol

1. On the course page, choose a ticket and select **Enrol**.
2. Check the **Attendee** details. They are filled in from your profile, and the confirmation is sent to the email shown.
3. Fill in any other fields the course asks for. If the course asks for **Documents**, upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo for each; photos are compressed to under 2 MB before upload.
4. Review the **Summary**, then:
   - **Enrol for free** for a free ticket, or
   - **Continue to payment** to pay now, or
   - **Save to cart, pay later** to keep the ticket in your [Order Cart](../billing/order-cart-and-transactions.md).

If you're already enrolled, or already have a seat waiting for payment, you can't enrol in the same course again. The course page shows **Complete payment** for a seat that is waiting.

Paid seats are held until the payment deadline shown on the course and in **My Courses**. Courses are paid on their own, separately from Lab plans and add-ons. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md).

## My Courses

**My Courses** shows your enrolments under **Active**, and cancelled or refunded ones under **Canceled & refunded**. Each enrolment has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | Your seat is held until the date shown. Select **Complete payment** to finish |
| **Enrolled** | Payment is confirmed (or the ticket was free) |
| **Canceled** | The enrolment was cancelled |
| **Refunded** | The payment was refunded |

Once you're enrolled in a course that has lessons online, the enrolment shows your progress as **x of y lessons** (or **Completed**) and a button to the lessons: **Start lessons**, **Continue lessons** once you've begun, or **Review lessons** when you've finished. See [Follow the lessons](#follow-the-lessons).

**Join meeting** and **Join community** links appear here when the course team shares them; they're also emailed to you before the course starts. **Copy meeting link** copies the link, for example to add to your calendar.

For refunds, see [Refunds & cancellation](../billing/refunds-and-cancellation.md).

## Follow the lessons

Courses with lessons online open in the lesson player. Go to **Courses → My Courses** and select **Start lessons**, **Continue lessons** or **Review lessons** on an enrolment with the status **Enrolled**. The player opens at the first lesson you haven't finished yet.

- **The header** shows the course title, your progress as **x of y lessons done** with a percentage and a progress bar, and **My courses** to go back.
- **The lesson list** groups lessons by topic. A tick marks a lesson you've done, a lock marks one that isn't open to you yet, and a question mark marks a quiz step. Select a lesson to open it.
- **The lesson** shows its video, its text and, under **Files**, any downloads that come with it. If the video can't play in the page, select **Open the video**.
- **Previous** and **Next** move between lessons.

### Track your progress

Select **Mark as done** when you've finished a lesson. Konfersi saves it to your account straight away and moves you on to the next lesson. The button then reads **Done**; select it again if you want to mark the lesson as not done.

Interactive quizzes aren't available yet. On a quiz step, review the material, then mark it done.

Your progress is kept per account, so you can stop at any time and pick up where you left off, on any device.

### Finish a course

When every lesson is marked done, the Console shows **Course completed. Well done!**. If the course awards a certificate, Konfersi issues it at that moment: **View certificate** appears in the player header, your enrolment in **My Courses** shows **Your certificate is ready**, and you get a notification and an email. For live courses, the certificate is issued after the course team records your attendance.

Marking a lesson as not done afterwards doesn't take a certificate away.

<scalar-callout type="neutral">If the player says **Lessons for this course are not online yet.**, the course team hasn't published its lessons. Live courses use the meeting links instead.</scalar-callout>

See [Certificates](certificates.md) to share, download and verify your certificate.
