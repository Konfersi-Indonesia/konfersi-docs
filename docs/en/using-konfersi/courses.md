Konfersi runs live and self-paced courses. You find them under **Courses** in the Console side menu:

- **Marketplace**: every course on offer.
- **My Courses**: the courses you have enrolled in, with their join links.

## Browse courses

**Marketplace** lists each course with its format, schedule and starting price. Use the filter to show all courses or only live ones.

| Format | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Self-paced** | Start any time once your enrolment is confirmed |
| **Live online** | Scheduled sessions held online |
| **In person** | Scheduled sessions at a venue |
| **Hybrid** | Online and in person |

Open a course to see **About this course**, the **Schedule** (times are shown in the course's time zone), the **Instructor**, **Frequently asked questions** and the **Tickets** on sale. A ticket may show how many seats are left, or be marked **Sold out**, **Sales closed** or **Opens soon**.

## Enrol

1. On the course page, choose a ticket and select **Enrol**.
2. Check the **Attendee** details. They are filled in from your profile, and the confirmation is sent to the email shown.
3. Fill in any other fields the course asks for. If the course asks for **Documents**, upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo for each; photos are compressed to under 2 MB before upload.
4. Review the **Summary**, then:
   - **Enrol for free** for a free ticket, or
   - **Continue to payment** to pay now, or
   - **Save to cart, pay later** to keep the ticket in your [Order Cart](../billing/order-cart-and-transactions.md).

If you're already enrolled, or already have a seat waiting for payment, you can't enrol in the same course again. The course page shows **Complete payment** for a seat that is waiting.

Paid seats are held until the payment deadline shown on the course and in **My Courses**. Courses are paid on their own, separately from Lab plans and add-ons. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md).

## My Courses

**My Courses** shows your enrolments under **Active**, and cancelled or refunded ones under **Canceled & refunded**. Each enrolment has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | Your seat is held until the date shown. Select **Complete payment** to finish |
| **Enrolled** | Payment is confirmed (or the ticket was free) |
| **Canceled** | The enrolment was cancelled |
| **Refunded** | The payment was refunded |

Once you're enrolled, **Join meeting** and **Join community** links appear here when the course team shares them; they're also emailed to you before the course starts. **Copy meeting link** copies the link, for example to add to your calendar.

For refunds, see [Refunds & cancellation](../billing/refunds-and-cancellation.md).
