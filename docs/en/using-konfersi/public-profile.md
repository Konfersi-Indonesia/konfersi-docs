Your public profile is a CV that lives at **io.konfersi.com/your-handle**. You decide what anyone can see, what people must ask you for, and what stays private. It stays private until you publish it.

Open it from **Public profile** in the Console side menu.

<scalar-callout type="info">Your public profile is separate from your **account profile** (name, photo, contact details for your Konfersi account). The photo on your public profile comes from your account; change it with **Change photo**.</scalar-callout>

## Set it up

The first time, **Guided setup** walks you through five steps. **Full editor** shows everything on one page; you can switch between the two at any time.

| Step | What you add |
|---|---|
| **Basics** | Your name, a headline (your role or focus) and, optionally, your location |
| **Links** | At least one way to reach you or find your work: website, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, ORCID, Google Scholar, DOI and more |
| **Summary** | A few sentences about what you do |
| **More sections** | Experience, education, publications, projects, skills, certifications, languages, awards, volunteering, or a section with your own title |
| **Address and publish** | Your handle, then publish |

Your name, a headline, one link or contact and a summary are required before you can publish. The progress bar shows how many are left.

Descriptions accept **Markdown**: headings, lists, links, bold, italic and tables.

Under **On Konfersi** you can show what you have done here: completed courses, your active certificates (each links to its verification page) and Lab projects you own. Pick the ones to show.

Select **Save changes** to keep your work. Unsaved changes are kept in your browser if you reload, and **Preview** shows exactly what a visitor will see.

## Choose who sees what

Every field and section has its own setting:

| Setting | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone with the link, and search engines once you publish |
| **On request** | Only people you approve, after they ask (see [Data requests](#data-requests)) |
| **Only me** | Nobody but you |

Your name is always public. By default your email, company email, phone number and location are **On request**, completed courses and Lab projects are **Only me**, and everything else is **Public**. A new entry shows its default until you save; change it whenever you like.

## Your address

Under **Your address**, choose a handle: 3–30 lowercase letters, numbers and single hyphens, for example `ana-sari`. Konfersi checks it as you type and suggests a free variant if it is taken. Some words are reserved for Konfersi itself.

You can change it later with **Change address**. Your old address keeps redirecting to the new one for 30 days, then it is released.

## Publish

Turn on **Publish profile** when you are ready. You confirm once that your public fields may be seen by anyone at your address and may appear in search engines; Konfersi records when you gave that consent.

Turn it off at any time. An unpublished profile shows visitors "Profile not found".

On your public page, visitors can **Share this profile**, scan its QR code, and **Save contact (vCard)**.

## Data requests

Fields you set to **On request** are listed on your page by name, not shown. A visitor who is signed in to Konfersi can select **Request access** and pick what they want to see. You get a notification.

In **Data requests**:

- **Requests to me**: choose which of the requested fields to share and for how long (30 days if you leave it empty, up to 365), then **Approve**, or **Decline**.
- **My requests**: requests you sent to others. **Withdraw request** while it is still pending.
- **Shared access**: everyone who can currently see your on-request fields, and until when. **Stop sharing** ends it at once; the person is told in Konfersi.

People only ever see the fields you approved, and only until the date shown.

## Your data

Under **Your data**:

- **Download my data** gives you a JSON file with your profile, the history of your addresses, and your requests and grants.
- **Delete profile** removes your profile content, requests and grants. Your address stops working and is released after the cooldown. Your Konfersi account stays.

See the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy.md) for how long data is kept.

## Share your profile

Once your profile is published, **Share your profile** gives you:

- **Share**: send your public page with your device's share sheet, or copy the link.
- **Show QR code**: a code people can scan to open your page. Download it as **QR code (SVG)** or **QR code (PNG)**.
- **Images for stories and posts**: **Story (1080×1920)** or **Post (1080×1080)**, a picture of your public profile with its QR code, ready for social media. Save your changes first; the image shows what is published.

Only your **Public** fields appear on the page and in the images.

## Download your CV

**Download your CV** creates an ATS-friendly CV from your profile: one column of real text with standard headings, so the applicant-tracking systems many employers use can read it in order.

1. Choose the **CV language**: English or Indonesian. Headings are translated; your own text stays as you wrote it.
2. Select **PDF** or **Markdown**.

<scalar-callout type="warning">Your own CV includes **everything** in your profile, also details set to **On request** or **Only me**. Check it before you send it to anyone. Save your changes first, so the CV has your latest text.</scalar-callout>

## Import from your CV

Already have a CV? **Import from your CV** reads it with AI and fills in a draft of your profile.

1. Under **Pay with**, choose which project's AI tokens pay for the import: one of your own projects, or a project of an organisation that pays for imports. Konfersi shows about how many AI tokens an import uses and how many the project has left.
2. Choose your **CV file**: PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to the size shown.
3. Select **Import with AI**.
4. In **Review the import**, check what was found. Where your profile already has a value, tick the ones to take from the CV. Then select **Add to my draft**.
5. Check the fields the AI was unsure of, then **Save changes** to keep them. Nothing is saved or published until you do.

The file is read once and never stored. A PDF without readable text (a scanned document) is refused before any AI is used, and you are not charged; upload it as an image instead. A long CV may be read only in part. You can import a limited number of times per hour.

## Business cards

You get a business card for each organisation you belong to. Open **Business cards** in the Console side menu.

- By default a card shows your public profile and updates with it. Turn on **Use my own text** to type the name, title, organisation, email, phone, website and location yourself.
- Pick a **Template** and a **Size**, then **Save card**.
- Download it as **PNG**, **Print PDF** (with 3 mm bleed and crop marks for a print shop) or **vCard**. Save the card first.

The QR code on the card opens your public profile, so it appears only once your profile is published. When someone scans it, the visit is counted as a card scan in your profile statistics.

## For organisation owners

Owners of an organisation can set, under the organisation's settings:

- **Organisation defaults** for business cards: the template and **Card colour** every member's card starts with. Members can still choose their own template.
- **AI CV import**: **Members can import CVs with AI**, paid from one of the organisation's projects (**Paid from**). Each import uses that project's AI tokens. Share a project with the organisation first.
