## 1. Outputs are decision support

1.1. The Services help you understand metocean, climate and environmental conditions and their likely effect on your plans. Outputs are **decision-support information**. They are intended to be used by competent people together with other information and professional judgement.

1.2. Outputs are **not**:

1. certified or stamped metocean studies, design criteria or design basis documents;
2. environmental impact assessments (including AMDAL) or documents for submission to a regulator;
3. navigation, routing or safety-of-life advice, or a replacement for official forecasts and warnings from the competent meteorological and maritime authorities;
4. a certification, approval or verification by a classification society, marine warranty surveyor or other certifying body;
5. legal, financial or insurance advice.

## 2. Model and data uncertainty

2.1. Outputs are produced from numerical models, reanalyses, satellite and in-situ observations and statistical methods. Third-party datasets such as ERA5, CMEMS, FES2022 and CMIP6 have their own resolution, coverage, biases and known limitations, which are described by their providers.

2.2. As a result, Outputs involve uncertainty. In particular:

1. historical statistics and hindcasts describe past conditions and do not guarantee future conditions;
2. estimated downtime, weather windows and exceedance probabilities depend on the location, period, thresholds and assumptions you enter;
3. local effects (for example, near-shore bathymetry, sheltering, local winds or currents) may not be resolved at the resolution of the underlying data;
4. climate projections describe scenarios, not predictions of specific events;
5. data may be incomplete, delayed or later revised by its provider.

2.3. We do not claim that any Metocean Data is exclusive to Konfersi, endorsed by any government, or equivalent to a certified site-specific measurement campaign or study.

## 3. AI-generated content

3.1. Some Outputs, such as report summaries and explanatory text, may be generated or assisted by artificial intelligence.

3.2. AI-generated text can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, can describe results with more confidence than the data supports, and can occasionally contain statements that are not supported by the underlying analysis. Always check AI-generated text against the underlying numbers, tables and charts before you rely on it or share it.

3.3. AI-generated content is not reviewed by a Konfersi specialist unless we have expressly agreed to do so in a separate consulting SOW.

## 4. Your responsibility

4.1. You are responsible for:

1. checking that the inputs, thresholds and assumptions you enter are correct and appropriate for your operation;
2. reviewing Outputs critically and, where the decision matters, having them checked by a qualified person;
3. applying applicable regulations, industry practice, company procedures and the instructions of competent authorities, which always take precedence over Outputs;
4. every operational, engineering, safety, commercial and investment decision you make.

4.2. Weather and sea conditions can change quickly. Always use current official forecasts and on-site observations for real-time operational and safety decisions.
