This notice explains how PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant ("**Konfersi**", "**we**") uses cookies and similar browser storage on konfersi.com, accounts.konfersi.com, app.konfersi.com (Console) and lab.konfersi.com (Lab). It supplements our [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

## 1. What cookies and browser storage are

1.1. A **cookie** is a small text file that a website stores in your browser and that is sent back to the website on later visits.

1.2. **Local storage** and **session storage** are similar browser features that let a website keep small amounts of data on your device. Session storage is cleared when you close the browser tab; local storage stays until you or the website clear it.

## 2. What we use them for

We use cookies and browser storage in two categories only:

- **Essential**: needed to sign you in, keep your session secure and move you between Konfersi apps. The Services cannot work without them.
- **Preferences**: remember choices you make, such as language and light or dark theme.

We do **not** currently use analytics, advertising or social media tracking cookies. We will update this notice, and ask for your consent where required, before introducing any.

## 3. Cookies we set

Cookies marked "all Konfersi sites" are set for the `.konfersi.com` domain so that you stay signed in across Accounts, Console and Lab. They are not shared with other domains.

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Category |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `konfersi_session` | Konfersi sign-in service, all Konfersi sites | Confirms you are signed in when a page loads. HttpOnly (not readable by page scripts). | 15 minutes | Essential |
| `konfersi_refresh` | Konfersi sign-in service, all Konfersi sites | Keeps you signed in by renewing your session. HttpOnly. | 30 days, or until you sign out | Essential |
| `konfersi_token` | Konfersi sign-in service and Konfersi apps, all Konfersi sites | Passes your short-lived sign-in token between Accounts, Console and Lab. | 15 minutes, or until you close the browser | Essential |
| `oauth_state` | Konfersi sign-in service | Protects sign-in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft against cross-site request forgery. HttpOnly. | 10 minutes | Essential |
| `oauth_link_user` | Konfersi sign-in service | Identifies your Account while you link a Google, GitHub or Microsoft sign-in to it. HttpOnly. | 10 minutes | Essential |
| `konfersi_lang` | Accounts and Console, all Konfersi sites | Remembers your language (English or Bahasa Indonesia). | 1 year | Preferences |
| `konfersi_theme` | Accounts and Console, all Konfersi sites | Remembers your light or dark theme. | 1 year | Preferences |

Konfersi staff who sign in to our internal administration tools also receive a separate `konfersi_admin_session` cookie (1 hour, essential). It is not set for customers.

## 5. Third-party services

When you make a payment in the Console, the payment window of our payment gateway, Midtrans, is loaded from Midtrans's own servers. Midtrans may use its own cookies or browser storage to process the payment securely and prevent fraud; this is governed by Midtrans's own policies.

If you choose to sign in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft, you are temporarily redirected to that provider, which may set its own cookies under its own policies.

## 6. Your choices

6.1. Because we currently use only essential and preference cookies and storage, we do not show a cookie consent banner. If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will first update this notice and give you a way to accept or refuse them, and to withdraw your choice later.

6.2. You can block or delete cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you will not be able to sign in. If you delete preference items, your language and theme will revert to the default.

## 7. Changes and contact

We will update this notice whenever we add, change or remove a cookie or storage item. The date at the top of this page shows the last update. For questions, contact support@konfersi.com or see [Contact support](../support/contact-support.md).
