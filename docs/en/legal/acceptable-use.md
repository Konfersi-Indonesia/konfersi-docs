This Acceptable Use Policy ("**Policy**") forms part of our [Terms of Service](terms.md). It applies to everyone who uses the Services of PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant ("**Konfersi**", "**we**"), including collaborators invited to a project. Capitalised terms have the meaning given in the Terms.

## 1. General principle

Use the Services lawfully, for their intended purpose, and in a way that does not harm Konfersi, other users, data providers or the public.

## 2. Data and Outputs

You must not:

1. scrape, crawl or use automated means to extract Metocean Data or Outputs from the Services, except through features or interfaces we provide for that purpose and within your Plan's limits;
2. bulk-download, redistribute, resell, sublicense or publish Metocean Data, or build a competing dataset or service from it;
3. breach the licence terms of third-party data providers (such as ERA5, CMEMS, FES2022 and CMIP6), including by removing required attributions;
4. present, sell or share Outputs as a study, report or dataset that is certified, stamped, approved or endorsed by Konfersi, or remove notices and disclaimers that we include in Outputs;
5. present Outputs as certified engineering, navigation, classification or environmental impact assessment advice (see our [AI & metocean disclaimer](ai-and-metocean-disclaimer.md));
6. use a non-commercial Plan for commercial work.

## 3. Accounts and access

You must not:

1. share your sign-in credentials, or let anyone else use your Account; invite colleagues as collaborators instead;
2. transfer or share an invitation that was issued to you;
3. create Accounts using false information or impersonate any person or organisation;
4. circumvent Plan limits, quotas, access controls or usage metering, for example by creating multiple Accounts.

## 4. Security

4.1. You must not:

1. probe, scan or test the vulnerability of the Services, or attempt to breach security or authentication measures, without our prior written permission;
2. access or attempt to access data, projects or Accounts that are not yours;
3. interfere with or disrupt the Services, including by denial-of-service attacks, excessive automated requests or introducing malware;
4. reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Services, except to the extent this restriction is prohibited by law.

4.2. If you find a security vulnerability, please report it to us in good faith following the responsible disclosure guidance on our [Security](../trust/security.md) page, and do not exploit or disclose it before we have had a reasonable opportunity to fix it.

## 5. Content and conduct

You must not use the Services or our community channels (including our Discord community and helpdesk) to upload, store or share content that:

1. is unlawful under Indonesian law or the law that applies to you, including content that infringes intellectual property, privacy or personal data rights;
2. is defamatory, harassing, hateful, obscene or threatening;
3. contains personal data of other people that you have no right to share, or specific personal data (such as health or biometric data) that is not needed for the Services;
4. is spam, unsolicited advertising or phishing;
5. contains confidential information of third parties that you have no right to disclose.

## 6. Lab compute fair use

6.1. Lab compute is provided for analysis related to metocean, climate and environmental work and for learning, within the compute hours included in your Plan.

6.2. You must not use Lab compute for unrelated or abusive workloads, such as cryptocurrency mining, password cracking, running public servers or proxies, sending bulk email, or attacking other systems.

6.3. We may throttle, pause or stop workloads that exceed your Plan's allocation, that degrade the Services for other users, or that breach this Policy. Where practical, we will tell you first and give you an opportunity to adjust.

## 7. Domains and brand

You must not register or use domain names, social media accounts or other identifiers that are confusingly similar to "Konfersi" to mislead users, or use our name and logos in a way that suggests an endorsement or partnership that does not exist.

## 8. Enforcement

8.1. If we reasonably believe that you have breached this Policy, we may, in proportion to the seriousness of the breach: remove or disable the relevant content; limit, suspend or stop workloads; suspend or terminate your access or Account under section 13 of the Terms; and report unlawful activity to the competent authorities.

8.2. Where reasonably practicable and lawful, we will notify you and give you an opportunity to explain or correct the breach before we act. We may act immediately where there is a risk to security, to other users or to legal compliance.

8.3. To report a breach of this Policy, contact support@konfersi.com.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Policy under section 20 of the Terms. For questions, see [Contact support](../support/contact-support.md).
