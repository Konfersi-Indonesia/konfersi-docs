This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you use the Konfersi websites, Accounts, Console, Lab, Mission Planning, courses and support channels (the "**Services**"). It is prepared with reference to Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (*UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi*, "**UU PDP**"). Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in our [Terms of Service](terms.md).

## 1. Who we are

1.1. The personal data controller (*Pengendali Data Pribadi*) for the Services is **PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant** ("**Konfersi**", "**we**", "**us**"), a limited liability company established under Indonesian law, NIB 2009240105369, Jl. Percetakan Negara No. C230, Rawasari, Jakarta Pusat, DKI Jakarta, Indonesia.

1.2. Until we appoint a dedicated data protection officer or privacy contact, privacy matters are handled by our **Direktur**. You can reach us at **support@konfersi.com** (please put "Privacy" in the subject line) or by post to the address above.

1.3. Where you use the Services on behalf of an organisation and upload personal data of other people (for example, colleagues invited to a project), your organisation may be the controller for that data and we process it on its behalf.

## 2. Personal data we collect

2.1. **Account and identity data**: email address; full name and display name; password (stored only as a one-way hash); email verification status; preferred language; profile photo (if you upload one); and an internal user code.

2.2. **Sign-in provider data**: if you choose to sign in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft, we receive from that provider your account identifier, email address, display name and profile photo URL, according to the permissions you approve.

2.3. **Registration profile data**: country; organisation name, sector and your role; organisation location and phone number (both optional); how you heard about Konfersi; the purposes for which you intend to use the Services; and your acceptance of our Terms.

2.4. **Project and Customer Data**: projects, tags and collaborator membership; email addresses of people you invite to a project; Mission Planning configurations; locations (latitude and longitude) and labels you add; analysis requests and results; and plan quota and usage records for your projects.

2.5. **Transaction data**: orders (plan, add-ons, status); payment records from our payment gateway (order reference, amount, payment method type, transaction and fraud-screening status, and the gateway's notification data); and, if you save a card, a card token with the masked card number and card brand. For course purchases, we also record the name and email address given at checkout, the amount, currency and payment status. We do not receive or store your full card number or card security code.

2.6. **Session and security data**: records of your sign-in sessions (stored as hashed tokens) with device information, creation, last-use and expiry times; short-lived sign-in state used to protect against cross-site request forgery; device sign-in sessions (for example, when you connect the Lab); and technical logs with request identifiers. Our hosting provider processes technical data such as IP address and browser information to deliver and protect traffic to the Services.

2.7. **Support and community data**: the content of support requests and your contact details when you email support@konfersi.com or use our helpdesk; and your username and messages if you join our Discord community.

2.8. **Course data**: the details you give when you register for a course, your enrolment and progress, and, where applicable, certificates issued to you.

2.9. **Cookies and similar technologies**: see our [Cookie Notice](cookies.md).

2.10. We do not intentionally collect specific personal data (*data pribadi yang bersifat spesifik*) as defined in UU PDP, such as health, biometric or criminal-record data. Please do not include such data in Customer Data or support requests.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data only for the purposes below, on the legal bases listed in Article 20 paragraph (2) of UU PDP.

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (UU PDP art. 20(2)) |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your Account, sign you in and keep it secure | Account, sign-in provider, session data | Performance of a contract (b) |
| Provide Console, Lab, Mission Planning and project collaboration | Project and Customer Data, Account data | Performance of a contract (b) |
| Process payments, issue invoices and handle refunds | Transaction data, Account data | Performance of a contract (b); legal obligation (c) for tax and accounting records |
| Deliver courses and certificates | Course data, Account data | Performance of a contract (b) |
| Respond to support requests and run the community | Support and community data | Performance of a contract (b); legitimate interest (f) |
| Detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents | Session, security and transaction data | Legitimate interest (f); legal obligation (c) |
| Understand how the Services are used in aggregate and improve them, using registration answers such as sector and purpose | Registration profile, usage data | Legitimate interest (f) |
| Send service messages (for example, sign-in, security, billing, changes to terms) | Account data | Performance of a contract (b); legal obligation (c) |
| Send newsletters, course announcements and marketing | Account data | Explicit consent (a), which you may withdraw at any time |
| Comply with law and requests from competent authorities, and establish or defend legal claims | Any relevant data | Legal obligation (c); legitimate interest (f) |

Where we rely on legitimate interest, we have balanced our interest against your rights. Where we rely on consent, we will ask for it clearly and separately.

## 5. Transfers outside Indonesia

5.1. Some of our service providers operate globally. In particular, Cloudflare serves the Services from a global network of data centres, and Discord, Google, GitHub and Microsoft are based outside Indonesia. Members of our team who work from Japan may access personal data to operate and support the Services.

5.2. When we transfer personal data outside Indonesia, we do so in accordance with Article 56 of UU PDP: we ensure the recipient country has an equal or higher level of protection, or that adequate and binding safeguards are in place, or, where neither applies, we obtain your consent.

## 6. How long we keep personal data

6.1. We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes in section 3, and then delete or anonymise it. As a default:

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account, registration profile and Customer Data | While your Account is active, then up to 30 days after closure to allow data export, after which it is deleted or anonymised within 90 days (except backups, which are overwritten on their normal cycle) |
| Transaction and invoice records | For the period required by Indonesian tax, accounting and company-document law, generally ten (10) years under Law No. 8 of 1997 on Company Documents |
| Sign-in session records | Until the session expires or is revoked (sign-in sessions last up to 30 days), then deleted in routine clean-up |
| Support requests | For as long as needed to resolve the request and for up to 3 years after it is closed |
| Technical and security logs | For short periods set by our hosting provider, unless needed to investigate an incident |
| Marketing preferences | Until you withdraw consent, after which we keep only a suppression record so we do not contact you again |

6.2. We may keep data longer where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 7. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and session tokens, access controls, and separation of production and non-production environments; non-production environments must not hold customer personal data. No system is completely secure. See [Security](../trust/security.md) for more detail. We do not claim any security certification unless it is stated on that page.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to UU PDP and its implementing regulations, you have the right to:

1. obtain information about the identity of the controller, the legal basis and purpose of processing, and our accountability (art. 5);
2. complete, update or correct inaccurate personal data about you (art. 6);
3. access and obtain a copy of your personal data (art. 7);
4. end processing and have your personal data deleted or destroyed (art. 8);
5. withdraw consent you have given (art. 9);
6. object to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (art. 10);
7. delay or restrict processing proportionately to its purpose (art. 11);
8. sue and receive compensation for violations in processing your personal data (art. 12); and
9. obtain and/or use your personal data in a commonly used or machine-readable format, and have it transmitted to another controller where technically possible (art. 13).

You can also lodge a complaint with the personal data protection authority established under UU PDP.

## 9. How to exercise your rights

9.1. Send your request to **support@konfersi.com** from the email address linked to your Account, with "Privacy request" in the subject line and a description of what you are asking for. You can correct most Account and profile data yourself in Accounts or the Console.

9.2. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will acknowledge your request and respond within the time limits set by UU PDP. If we cannot fully meet a request (for example, because we must keep transaction records by law), we will tell you why.

9.3. Exercising your rights is free of charge, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

## 10. Personal data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected data subjects and the personal data protection authority in writing no later than **3 × 24 hours** after we become aware of it, as required by Article 46 of UU PDP. The notice will describe the data involved, when and how the breach occurred, and the steps we are taking to handle and recover from it.

## 11. Children

The Services are intended for users aged 18 and over. Some courses may be attended by students under 18; in that case, we process their personal data only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, as required by Article 25 of UU PDP. If you believe we have collected a child's personal data without such consent, please contact us so we can delete it.

## 12. Automated processing and AI

12.1. Mission Planning and other modules analyse Customer Data and Metocean Data automatically, and some Outputs may include AI-generated text. These analyses concern environmental and operational conditions; we do not use them to make decisions about individuals that have legal or similarly significant effects.

12.2. We do not use your Customer Data to train AI models made available to other customers without telling you first and, where required, obtaining your consent.

12.3. See our [AI & metocean disclaimer](ai-and-metocean-disclaimer.md) for the limits of automated Outputs.

## 13. Marketing communications

We send marketing emails only with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in the email or by contacting support@konfersi.com. Service messages about your Account, security, billing or changes to our terms are not marketing, and you will keep receiving them while your Account is active.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. For material changes, we will notify you by email or in the Services at least 14 days before they take effect, and ask for new consent where UU PDP requires it. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
