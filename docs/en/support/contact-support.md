Need a hand? Here is how to reach us and how to get the quickest answer.

## Ways to reach us

| Channel | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Email: [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com)** | Account, sign-in, billing, refund, data and bug reports. Each email opens a ticket in our helpdesk, so the whole team can follow it up |
| **Discord community** | Questions for other users, tips on Lab and datasets, and product announcements. Ask us for an invite at support@konfersi.com |

For anything involving your account, payments or personal data, use email rather than Discord. Never post passwords, tokens, order IDs or payment details in a public channel.

## What to include in your message

Including these details helps us answer without asking follow-up questions:

- **Your account email**: the address you use to sign in at accounts.konfersi.com
- **Where it happened**: Console (app.konfersi.com), Lab (lab.konfersi.com), sign-in, or the website
- **Project name**, if the issue involves a specific Console project
- **What you did, what you expected and what happened instead**
- **Screenshots or the exact error message**
- **Date, time and your time zone**
- **Browser and operating system**, for display or sign-in issues
- **Order ID**, for anything about payments. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md#something-went-wrong)

If an error screen shows a request ID or reference code, include it. It lets us find the exact request in our logs.

## Response expectations

We answer support emails in the order they arrive, as quickly as we can. Replies may take longer on weekends and Indonesian public holidays.

Some plans describe a specific level of support. For example, the **Professional & Projects** tier is described as including next-business-day email support. Enterprise support terms are set out in your organisation's agreement. See [Plans & pricing](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).

## Privacy requests

To access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent, email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com). The [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy.md) explains your rights and how we handle these requests.

## Reporting a security issue

If you think you've found a security vulnerability, **don't post it on Discord or anywhere public**. Email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) with the subject line **"Security"**. See [Security](../trust/security.md#reporting-a-vulnerability) for what to include.
