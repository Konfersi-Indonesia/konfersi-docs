Quick answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, [contact support](contact-support.md).

## Account and sign-in

### What is Konfersi?

Konfersi (PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant) provides metocean, climate and environmental intelligence for offshore, coastal, energy and infrastructure projects. We deliver it through digital platforms, courses and expert consulting. See [What is Konfersi?](../getting-started/what-is-konfersi.md) and [About Konfersi](../company/about.md).

### Do I need separate accounts for the Console and Lab?

No. One Konfersi account works across every app. You sign in once at accounts.konfersi.com and can then use the Console (app.konfersi.com) and Lab (lab.konfersi.com). See [Products & surfaces](../getting-started/products-and-surfaces.md).

### Can I sign in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft?

Yes. Where social sign-in is enabled, the sign-in page shows these options alongside email and password. See [Sign in](../getting-started/sign-in.md). To create an account first, see [Create an account](../getting-started/create-account.md).

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you. The link works only once and expires, so request a new one if it has stopped working. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then [contact support](contact-support.md).

## Plans and payments

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. The **Academic & Research** tier is free and aimed at students, researchers and academics, for non-commercial use. New Console projects start on the free plan. See [Plans & pricing](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).

### Where can I see prices?

Current prices are shown in the Console under **Manage Plan**, and at checkout. Prices appear in IDR for organisations in Indonesia and in USD elsewhere. Payments are processed in IDR.

### Is a plan per account or per project?

Per project. Each Console project has its own plan and resource quotas (storage, compute and AI credits). You can top up a single project with add-ons.

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit or debit card, ATM/bank transfer through a virtual account, and QRIS / e-wallets such as GoPay. All are processed by Midtrans. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md).

### My payment went through but my plan didn't change. What now?

Open **Manage Plan → Transactions** and check the order status. If it still shows **In Progress** a few minutes after you paid, or you were charged but the order failed, email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) with your order ID. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md#something-went-wrong).

### How do I get a refund?

Email support@konfersi.com with your order ID and the details listed in [Refunds & cancellation](../billing/refunds-and-cancellation.md). The rules that apply are in the [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md).

## Data, Lab and Mission Planning

### Where does Konfersi's metocean data come from?

Konfersi works with established global datasets. Our analyses are calibrated and validated against sources such as ERA5, CMEMS, FES2022 and CMIP6. See [Metocean data sources](../using-konfersi/metocean-data-sources.md). Outputs support your decision-making, but they don't replace a site-specific certified study. Read the [AI & metocean disclaimer](../legal/ai-and-metocean-disclaimer.md).

### What is Lab?

Lab is Konfersi's workspace for working with ocean and climate data. It is available at lab.konfersi.com. Monthly Lab compute depends on your plan. See [Lab workspace](../using-konfersi/lab-workspace.md).

### Is Mission Planning available yet?

Mission Planning is rolling out. It is the first module of the Konfersi metocean platform to be released externally, and the launch is planned for the end of October 2026. It runs inside a Console project: you set the location and period, run the analysis, and get downtime results and a report. Details may change before launch. See [Mission Planning](../using-konfersi/mission-planning.md).

### Can other people work on my project?

Yes. A project owner can invite collaborators to a Console project, and only the owner can invite or remove them. See [Console & projects](../using-konfersi/console-projects.md).

## Privacy and security

### How is my personal data used?

We process your personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP). The [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy.md) explains what we collect, why, and your rights, including access, correction and deletion. For cookies, see the [Cookie Notice](../legal/cookies.md).

### How do I report a security problem?

Email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) with the subject line "Security", and don't share the details publicly. See [Security](../trust/security.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
