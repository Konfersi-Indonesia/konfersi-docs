Quick answers to the questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, [contact support](contact-support.md).

## Account and sign-in

### What is Konfersi?

Konfersi (PT Konfersi Metocean Climate Consultant) provides metocean, climate and environmental intelligence for offshore, coastal, energy and infrastructure projects. We deliver it through digital platforms, courses and expert consulting. See [What is Konfersi?](../getting-started/what-is-konfersi.md) and [About Konfersi](../company/about.md).

### Do I need separate accounts for different Konfersi apps?

No. One Konfersi account works across every app. You sign in once at accounts.konfersi.com and can then use the Console (app.konfersi.com). See [Products & surfaces](../getting-started/products-and-surfaces.md).

### Can I sign in with Google, GitHub or Microsoft?

Yes. Where social sign-in is enabled, the sign-in page shows these options alongside email and password. See [Sign in](../getting-started/sign-in.md). To create an account first, see [Create an account](../getting-started/create-account.md).

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

Choose **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you. The link works only once and expires, so request a new one if it has stopped working. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then [contact support](contact-support.md).

## Courses

### How do I enrol in a course?

In the Console, open **Courses → Marketplace**, open a course, choose a ticket and select **Enroll**. Fill in the attendee details, then pay or, for a free ticket, enrol straight away. See [Courses](../using-konfersi/courses.md).

### Where do I find my courses and certificates?

Your enrolments are under **Courses → My Courses**, with their status, lesson progress and join links. Certificates you earn are under **Courses → Certificates**. See [Certificates](../using-konfersi/certificates.md).

## Payments

### Where can I see prices?

Current prices are shown in the Console on each course and at checkout. Every price is shown in your account's billing currency, which is set from your country when you sign up (for example, IDR for Indonesia). Only support can change it. Payments are processed in IDR through Midtrans; payment in USD isn't available yet. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md#currency-tax-and-current-prices).

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit or debit card, ATM/bank transfer through a virtual account, and QRIS / e-wallets such as GoPay. All are processed by Midtrans. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md).

### Can I pay for several courses at once?

Yes. Save the course tickets to your Order Cart, then select them and check out together in one payment. See [Order cart & transactions](../billing/order-cart-and-transactions.md).

### My payment went through but my enrolment didn't change. What now?

Open **Payment & Billing → Transactions** and check the order status. If it still shows **Awaiting Payment** a few minutes after you paid, or you were charged but the order failed, email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) with your order ID. See [Payments](../billing/payments.md#something-went-wrong).

### How do I get a refund?

Email support@konfersi.com with your order ID and the reason for your request. See [Contact support](contact-support.md). The rules that apply are in the [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md).

## Privacy and security

### How is my personal data used?

We process your personal data in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP). The [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy.md) explains what we collect, why, and your rights, including access, correction and deletion. For cookies, see the [Cookie Notice](../legal/cookies.md).

### How do I report a security problem?

Email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) with the subject line "Security", and don't share the details publicly. See [Security](../trust/security.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
