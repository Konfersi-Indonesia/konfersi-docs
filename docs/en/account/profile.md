Open **Profile & Settings → My Profile** in the Console side menu. Your profile is shared across Konfersi: the Console, Lab and other Konfersi services all use it.

## Photos

- **Profile photo**: select **Change profile photo** to upload a JPG, PNG or WebP photo, cropped to a square before it's saved, or **Delete Profile Picture** to remove it.
- **Cover photo**: select **Add cover photo** or **Change cover**. Use **Reposition** to drag it into place, or **Remove cover**.

## Edit your details

Select **Edit Profile**, make your changes, then **Save Changes**. **Discard** leaves without saving.

### Personal Information

**Full Name** and **Country** are required. **Phone Number** is optional. Your sign-in email is shown but can't be changed here.

### Organization Information

All organization fields are optional: organization name, type, sector, size, website, location and your **Role**.

If you belong to an [organization](../using-konfersi/organizations/index.md) on Konfersi, you can use its details instead of typing them:

- Choose the organization at the top of the section. Its name, type, sector, size, website and location are then shown read-only and kept up to date by the organization's owners. Only your **Role** stays yours to edit.
- Choose **Enter details myself** to type them instead.

When you join an organization and your profile isn't using one yet, it starts using the new organization's details automatically. If you leave, your profile keeps the details it had, and you can edit them again.

Invited to an organization but not a member yet? Accept the invitation first. The edit page links to **Review invitations**.

### How You Use Konfersi

**How did you hear about Konfersi?** and **What will you use Konfersi for?** are required, as they were when you signed up. Pick from the options or type your own.

If a required field is missing, for example on an older account, your profile shows a reminder with a link to complete it.

## Preferences

Under **Preferences**, choose your **Language** and **Appearance** (**Light** or **Dark**). They apply across Konfersi on every device you sign in to.

## Connect sign-in providers

Under **Connect Social Links**, select **Connect** next to a provider, such as Google, GitHub or Microsoft, to sign in with it as well. If that provider account already belongs to another Konfersi account, you can ask support to merge them.

Accounts that sign in only through a provider can't change their email or password in Konfersi.

## Change your password

Select **Change Password**. Your password must meet the length shown, and a strength meter suggests how to make it stronger. See also [Security](security/index.md).

## Delete your account

Select **Delete Account**, type `DELETE MY ACCOUNT` (or `HAPUS AKUN SAYA` if the Console is in Bahasa Indonesia) and select **Confirm Delete Account**. Konfersi emails you a confirmation link:

1. Select the link within **24 hours** to schedule the deletion. Your account is deactivated straight away.
2. Your data is permanently deleted **30 days** later.

Projects you created are left without an owner. [Contact support](../support/contact-support.md) to transfer them first, or to recover your account if you deleted it by mistake.
