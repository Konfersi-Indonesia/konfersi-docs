This page explains, in practical terms, how to cancel an order and how to ask for a refund. The binding rules are in the refund section of our [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md). If this page and the Terms ever disagree, the Terms apply.

<scalar-callout type="warning">Our refund policy is pending final legal review. The process on this page is how we handle requests today, but eligibility rules and time windows may change when the Terms are finalised. Always check the current [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md).</scalar-callout>

## Cancelling before you pay

You don't need a refund for an order you haven't paid yet:

- **Unpaid orders** can be left alone. A pending payment expires automatically if it isn't completed in time, and you are not charged.
- **Failed, expired or canceled orders** are never charged. Their status in **Payment & Billing → Transactions** shows this.

See [Payments](payments.md) for what each status means.

## Courses

This covers Konfersi Course Series, Web Series and Private Course purchases.

- Whether a course purchase can be refunded depends on the course type, timing, and how much of the course you have already accessed. Those conditions are set out in the [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md).
- A **Private Course** is scheduled and tailored for you, so changes and cancellations are usually agreed with the course team directly. Get in touch as early as you can.

## Platform plans and add-ons

This covers plans and add-ons bought for a Console project.

- Plan and add-on resources, such as storage, compute and AI credits, are applied to your project as soon as payment is confirmed. The [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md) set out when a platform purchase can be refunded once those resources are available to you.
- If you were **charged twice**, or charged but your order never completed, that is a payment problem rather than a refund request. Contact us and we'll investigate. See [Payments](payments.md#something-went-wrong).
- **Enterprise & Operations** agreements follow the cancellation and refund terms in your signed agreement.

## How to request a refund

Email [support@konfersi.com](mailto:support@konfersi.com) from the email address on your Konfersi account or course registration. Use a subject line like "Refund request – [your order ID]" and include:

| Include | Why |
|---|---|
| **Order ID** | So we can find the transaction. For platform orders it's shown in **Transactions** and starts with `ord_`. For courses, use the order ID in your registration or payment email |
| **What you bought** | The plan, add-on or course name, and the project name for platform orders |
| **Payment date and method** | For example, "QRIS, 12 September 2026" |
| **Reason for the request** | A short explanation helps us resolve it faster |
| **Proof of payment** | A screenshot of your bank, card or e-wallet confirmation, if you have one |

**What happens next**

1. We confirm we've received your request and may ask for more details.
2. We review it against the [Terms of Service](../legal/terms.md) and let you know the outcome by email.
3. If a refund is approved, it is returned through the payment channel, or another method we agree with you. How long it takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

## After a refund

When a refund is made, Konfersi updates your order and tells you:

| Refund | In the Console | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Full refund of a plan or add-on** | The transaction shows **Refunded** and **Payment refunded**: *This payment was refunded and what the order granted has been removed from the project.* | The plan or add-on resources from that order are removed from the project |
| **Full refund of a course** | The transaction shows **Refunded** with *This payment was refunded and the course seat released.* The enrolment moves to **Canceled & refunded** in [My Courses](../using-konfersi/courses.md#my-courses) | Your seat is released |
| **Partial refund** | The transaction shows **Partially Refunded**: *Part of this payment was refunded. The order itself stays active; contact support if you have questions.* | Nothing else changes |

You also get a notification (**Payment Refunded**, **Course refunded** or **Partial refund**) in the Console and, unless you turned it off, by email. See [Notifications](../account/notifications.md).

The money goes back through the payment channel you used, or by the method agreed with you. The transaction in **Payment & Billing → Transactions** keeps a record of the refund.
