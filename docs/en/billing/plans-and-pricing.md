Konfersi plans are built around how much of the platform you use: Lab compute, storage, AI credits and access to the Metocean Analytics tools. This page explains the tiers, how plans and add-ons work inside the Console, and where you can see current prices.

<scalar-callout type="info">Pricing is still being finalised. The plan names and features below reflect how Konfersi positions each tier today. The Console always shows the plans and prices that currently apply, so check there before you buy.</scalar-callout>

## The three tiers

| Tier | Who it's for | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Academic & Research** | Students, individual researchers and academics exploring oceanographic datasets | Always free. Basic public BGC-Argo datasets, community learning platform, 10 hours/month of sandboxed Lab compute, community support, non-commercial licence |
| **Professional & Projects** | Engineering consultants, site screeners and small-to-medium offshore projects | Full Metocean Analytics suite, high-resolution wind and wave models, 100 hours/month of Lab and compute, ERA5 hindcast downloads, next-business-day email support |
| **Enterprise & Operations** | Major operators, government bodies and multi-site maritime deployments | Dedicated cloud compute, custom API and pipeline integrations, custom legal and data licensing. Tailored by proposal |

For **Enterprise & Operations**, contact us for a custom proposal. The scope, support terms and licensing are agreed in writing with your organisation. See [Contact support](../support/contact-support.md).

Courses (Konfersi Course Series, Web Series and Private Course) are sold separately from platform plans. See [Refunds & cancellation](refunds-and-cancellation.md) for how course purchases are handled.

## How plans work in the Console

Plans are applied **per project**, not per account. Every project you create in the [Console](../using-konfersi/console-projects.md) has its own plan and its own resource quotas.

- **New projects** start on the free plan by default. You can also pick a paid plan while you create the project.
- **Upgrading** a project moves it to a higher plan. The Console won't let you buy the plan the project is already on.
- **Add-ons** top up a single resource for a project, for example extra storage or extra AI credits, without changing its plan.

Each plan and add-on grants a set amount of one or more resources:

| Resource | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Storage | Cloud storage for your project's files, in GB |
| Compute | Compute credits for running analyses and Lab workloads |
| AI credits | Credits used by AI-assisted features |

When a payment completes, the plan's or add-on's resources are added to the project's quotas automatically.

## Where to manage your plan

In the Console side menu:

| Menu item | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Lab Project → Project Plans & Add-ons** | Compare plans, choose a plan for a project, and buy add-ons for extra resources |
| **Payment & Billing → Order Cart** | Review several plan or add-on orders before paying |
| **Payment & Billing → Transactions** | Track the status of your orders and continue a pending payment |

See [Order cart & transactions](order-cart-and-transactions.md).

## See plans on the Konfersi website

The **Pricing** page on the Konfersi website (select **Pricing** in the top bar) lists the same plans and prices as the Console, read live from the same plan catalog. You don't need an account to see it.

- Each plan shows its price, or **Free**, or **Custom pricing**, and what it includes, such as compute hours, storage and AI credits.
- Prices are in **IDR** when the website is in Indonesian and in **USD** in English. The note under the plans gives the VAT (PPN) that is added at checkout.
- **Get started** opens the Console's **Project Plans & Add-ons** page. If you are signed out, it opens **Create Account** instead. Once your account is set up, open **Lab Project → Project Plans & Add-ons** in the Console (see [Create an account](../getting-started/create-account.md)).
- **Contact sales** (on plans with custom pricing) opens a support request in the Console with the plan already filled in. Sign in first if you are asked to.

The price you pay is always the one shown at checkout in the Console.

## Currency, tax and current prices

- **Current prices are shown in the Console at checkout.** This documentation deliberately doesn't list numbers, because they can change.
- Prices are shown in **Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)** if your organisation's country is Indonesia, and in **US Dollars (USD)** otherwise. The currency follows the country in your profile.
- Payments themselves are processed in **IDR** through our payment partner, Midtrans. If you view prices in USD, treat the USD figure as an estimate. The IDR amount at checkout is what you are charged.
- Value-added tax (PPN) is added on top of the subtotal. The checkout breakdown shows the subtotal, tax and grand total before you pay.

To learn how paying works, see [Payments](payments.md).
