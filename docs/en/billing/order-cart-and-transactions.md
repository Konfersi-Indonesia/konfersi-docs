Both pages are under **Payment & Billing** in the Console side menu. The menu shows how many items are waiting in your cart.

## Order Cart

The **Order Cart** keeps the course tickets you saved to pay for later. You save a ticket in either of two ways (see [Courses](../using-konfersi/courses.md)):

- **Add to cart** next to a ticket on the course page
- **Save to cart, pay later** on the enrol page

The cart holds one ticket per course. Choosing **Switch cart to this ticket** on another ticket of the same course replaces the one you saved.

Each saved course shows its current price. The cart also tells you when a course can't be bought as saved, for example:

- **Price changed since you added it**, with the old price.
- **You already ordered this course. Complete the payment to keep the seat.** Select **Complete payment** to open that order.
- **You are already enrolled in this course.**
- **Enrolment for this course is closed.** or **This course is no longer available.**
- **This ticket is no longer on sale.** Select **Choose a ticket** to pick another one on the course page.

Only courses that can still be bought can be selected. To take a course out of the cart, select **Remove from cart**.

### Pay for saved courses

1. Tick the courses you want to pay for, or **Select All**.
2. Check the **Order summary**: the courses subtotal, tax (PPN) and **Total**.
3. Select **Checkout**.
4. On the **Checkout** page, fill in the attendee details and any documents for each course, choose a payment method, and select **Confirm & pay**.
5. Konfersi creates one order for everything you selected and opens it in **Transactions**, where the payment window opens. See [Payments](payments.md).

Everything you select is paid in one payment, in your billing currency (see [Payments](payments.md#your-billing-currency)). If saved items were priced in different currencies, the cart asks you to remove the older ones and add them again.

## Transactions

**Transactions** lists every order you placed, including course orders, with its payment status. Use the **Status** filter to show only some orders, and select **Load more** to see older ones.

- **Continue Payment** reopens a pending payment before it expires; the countdown shows how long is left.
- **View Details** opens the order.

The order page shows the order ID, the items, the amounts with tax, the payment method, and when the order was created, paid or must be paid by (**Pay before**). From there you can:

- finish a pending payment, **Change Payment Method** or **Cancel Order**
- **Refresh status** if the payment result hasn't arrived yet
- **Print receipt** once the order is completed

If an order was paid together with others in one checkout, the page says **Paid together with other products in one payment.** and **Open that payment** takes you to it.

For what each status means, see [Payments](payments.md#payment-and-order-statuses). Your enrolment status is also shown in **Courses → My Courses**.
